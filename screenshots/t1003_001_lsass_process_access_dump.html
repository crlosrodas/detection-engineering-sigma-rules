Detects a process opening a handle to lsass.exe (Sysmon EID 10 ProcessAccess) with a
GrantedAccess mask that grants the read/clone rights used to dump LSASS memory
(e.g. PROCESS_VM_READ | PROCESS_QUERY_INFORMATION). Covers the well-known dump masks used by
tools such as comsvcs.dll MiniDump, Mimikatz and ProcDump. GrantedAccess is compared
case-insensitively, so both upper- and lower-case hex representations are matched.
